# Platform Development Plan

## Sequence

1. canonical corpus and schemas;
2. source intelligence;
3. permission model;
4. knowledge audit core;
5. eval/experiment core;
6. exact and lexical knowledge retrieval;
7. deterministic guidance resolver;
8. CLI;
9. read-only MCP;
10. retrieval experiments;
11. skill registry;
12. enterprise adapter framework;
13. security-assurance pilot;
14. operational pilot;
15. future SDLC control-plane re-engineering.

## Governing rule

Do not jump ahead because a later subsystem is more interesting. Each stage should create evidence that informs the next.
